Privacy Policy for Shadow Discipline

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

Shadow Discipline ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") respects the privacy of its users ("you" or "User"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, protect, and respect your information when you use our mobile application, Shadow Discipline.

TL;DR: Shadow Discipline collects NO personal data. Everything stays on your phone. 100% offline. 100% private. Period.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Data We Collect

Short answer: We collect ZERO personal data.

Shadow Discipline does NOT collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your location data
  • Your contacts
  • Your browsing history
  • Your device ID or IMEI
  • Your IP address
  • Your advertising ID
  • Any other personally identifiable information (PII)

2.2 Habit Data (Local Only)

The ONLY data Shadow Discipline stores is:

  • Habits you create (name, description, time)
  • Completions you log (timestamps, XP earned)
  • Your current rank, level, and progression
  • Items you've unlocked (companions, shields, scrolls)
  • Mood entries you record
  • Streaks and achievement data

WHERE THIS DATA IS STORED:

  • Only on YOUR device (phone)
  • In local SQLite database
  • Never transmitted anywhere
  • Never synchronized to cloud
  • Never shared with anyone
  • Only you can access it

2.3 Permissions We Request

Shadow Discipline requests these permissions:

  • Notifications: To remind you of habits (you control this)
  • Alarms and Reminders: For daily notifications

We do NOT request:

  • Camera
  • Photos/Media
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Calendar
  • Phone calls
  • SMS/Messages
  • Fine location
  • Coarse location

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the habit data you create to:

  • Display your habits in the app
  • Calculate XP and rewards
  • Determine your rank and progression
  • Generate analytics and insights via the Sage Advisor
  • Create performance charts and statistics
  • Provide notifications and reminders

We do NOT use your data to:

  • Track you
  • Profile you
  • Sell to third parties
  • Serve advertisements
  • Train AI models
  • Share with social media
  • Analyze for marketing
  • Build personal profiles

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We NEVER share your data because we never collect it.

Your habit data is:

  • Stored 100% locally on your device
  • Not transmitted to our servers (we have none)
  • Not shared with third parties
  • Not shared with Google, Facebook, or any advertiser
  • Not used for any purpose outside the app

Your data remains YOUR data, exclusively.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • SQLite Encryption: Database uses SQLite standard encryption
  • Local Storage: Data never leaves your device
  • Atomic Transactions: Data consistency is guaranteed
  • No Network Transmission: No risk of interception
  • No Accounts: No password = no account breach risk
  • No Servers: No third-party servers = no hacks of cloud data
  • SQL Injection Protection: All queries are parameterized

Since your data never leaves your phone, it's protected by your device's native security (fingerprint, face recognition, PIN, pattern lock).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

You can delete your data:

  • Permanently: Uninstall the app to delete all data from your device
  • Selectively: Delete individual habits within the app
  • Archive: Archive habits to hide them but keep data

You can export your data:

  • Export all habits, completions, and stats as JSON file
  • Keep your own backup copy anytime
  • No dependency on us to keep your history

Once deleted from your device, data is permanently gone. We have no copy because we never stored it.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do NOT use:

  • Cookies
  • Web beacons
  • Pixels
  • Local storage
  • Analytics tools
  • Tracking pixels
  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase
  • Mixpanel
  • Any third-party tracking

The app has no internet connection requirement and contains no tracking code.

8. Third-Party Services

Shadow Discipline does NOT use third-party services that access your data:

  • No Google Play Services for analytics
  • No Firebase
  • No Sentry
  • No Crash reporting services
  • No Ads networks
  • No Social media SDKs

The ONLY third-party libraries used are:

  • React Native: App framework (MIT License)
  • Expo: Development platform (MIT License)
  • SQLite: Database (Public Domain)

None of these libraries have access to your habit data.

9. Advertising and Marketing

No ads. No marketing. No sponsors.

Shadow Discipline contains:

  • ZERO advertisements
  • ZERO marketing trackers
  • ZERO affiliate links
  • ZERO sponsored content
  • ZERO data sales to advertisers

We are not paid to track you or sell your data.

10. Children's Privacy

Shadow Discipline is NOT designed for children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used Shadow Discipline:

  • No data has been collected about them
  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Contact us if you have concerns

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any data from anyone, including children.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA, and others):

You have the right to:

  • Access: Export your data anytime (via app export feature)
  • Deletion: Delete your data by uninstalling the app
  • Portability: Move your data by exporting to JSON
  • No profiling: We don't create profiles of you
  • No automated decision-making: All decisions are yours alone
  • No marketing: We don't send marketing emails
  • No tracking: We don't track you across apps or websites

To exercise these rights, simply uninstall the app or export your data.

12. Compliance with Laws

Shadow Discipline complies with:

  • GDPR: European Union data protection (no data to protect)
  • CCPA: California Consumer Privacy Act (no data to process)
  • COPPA: Children's Online Privacy Protection (no children's data)
  • PIPEDA: Canadian privacy law (no data transmission)
  • Android Privacy Policy Requirements: All data local, no sharing
  • Apple Privacy Requirements: No tracking, no data collection

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect:

  • Changes to our privacy practices
  • New legal requirements
  • Clarifications to existing policies

We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Shadow Discipline after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Since Shadow Discipline collects no data, major changes are unlikely.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:

Contact Information:

  • Email: privacy@shadowdiscipline.local
  • App: Use the Settings → Support option within the app
  • GitHub: https://github.com/yourusername/shadow-discipline

Since we don't collect personal data, we may not be able to identify you, but we'll do our best to help with your inquiry.

15. Summary

The absolute bottom line:

  • ✓ Your data stays on your phone
  • ✓ We never collect personal information
  • ✓ We never sell your data
  • ✓ We never track you
  • ✓ We never share with anyone
  • ✓ You can delete anytime
  • ✓ You can export anytime
  • ✓ 100% offline, 100% private

Shadow Discipline is built on the principle that your discipline is your business. This Privacy Policy isn't a legal trick—it's the truth. We don't collect data. We don't track you. Your phone, your data, your discipline.
