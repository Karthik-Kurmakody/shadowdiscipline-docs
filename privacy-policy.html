Privacy Policy for Shadow Discipline

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

Shadow Discipline respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

TL;DR: Shadow Discipline collects NO personal data. Everything stays on your phone. 100% offline. 100% private.

2. Information We Collect

Short answer: We collect ZERO personal data.

Shadow Discipline does NOT collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personally identifiable information.

Habit Data (Local Only)

The ONLY data stored is:

  • Habits you create
  • Completions you log
  • Your rank and XP
  • Items and achievements

WHERE: Only on YOUR device, in local SQLite database. Never transmitted.

3. Permissions We Request

Shadow Discipline requests:

  • Notifications: To remind you of habits (you can disable anytime)

We do NOT request camera, location, contacts, calendar, or SMS permissions.

4. Data Sharing

We NEVER share your data because we never collect it.

Your habit data is stored 100% locally on your device. Not transmitted, not shared with anyone.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Local SQLite database storage
  • Atomic transactions for consistency
  • SQL injection protection
  • No network transmission = no interception risk
  • Your device's native security (PIN, biometric lock)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete: Uninstall the app to delete all data
  • Export: Export all your data as JSON anytime
  • Control: You own your data, you control it

7. Contact Us

Email: support@shadowdiscipline.local

Since we don't collect personal data, we may not be able to identify you, but we'll do our best to help.
