Privacy Policy for Shadow Discipline

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Shadow Discipline ("we," "us," or "our") is built with a privacy-first approach. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Shadow Discipline mobile application.

Summary: Shadow Discipline is designed to operate without collecting or transmitting personal data. Your data stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Data

We do not collect personal data such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers (e.g., IMEI)

2.2 App Data (Stored Locally Only)

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Habits (name, description, schedule)
  • Completion logs (timestamps, XP)
  • Progression data (levels, ranks)
  • Unlockable items
  • Mood entries
  • Streaks and achievements

This data is:

  • Stored only on your device
  • Not transmitted to any servers
  • Not shared with any third parties

2.3 Permissions

Shadow Discipline does not request any device permissions.

The app operates fully offline and does not require access to:

  • Camera
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Messages (SMS)
  • Storage
  • Microphone

3. How Data Is Used

All data remains on your device and is used only to provide app functionality, including:

  • Displaying your habits
  • Tracking progress and streaks
  • Calculating levels and rewards
  • Generating insights and statistics

We do not use your data for tracking, advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. Data Sharing

We do not collect or share user data. No data is transmitted outside your device.

5. Data Security

Your data remains on your device and is protected by your device’s built-in security (such as PIN, password, or biometric authentication).

While we aim to follow best practices, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete data within the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data

Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.

7. Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking technologies.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use general-purpose libraries such as React Native, Expo, and SQLite. These operate locally and do not transmit user data.

9. Advertising

Shadow Discipline contains no advertisements, sponsored content, or affiliate links.

10. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal data from users, including children. If concerned, simply uninstall the app to remove all data.

11. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected:

  • You control all your data locally
  • You can delete it anytime
  • You can export it (if feature available)

12. Legal Compliance

This app is designed to align with GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA by not collecting personal data.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date.
